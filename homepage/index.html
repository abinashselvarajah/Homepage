Computervision

Industrial recognition systems often lack enough real image data, so virtual data is a promising alternative. In my thesis, I investigated how well synthetic images work for training such systems.

Starting from a real image dataset of representative 3D models of Makeblock parts, I generated synthetic images in Unity using the Perception tool, with several randomizers to produce a sufficiently large and varied dataset. I then trained multiple neural networks on different mixes of real and synthetic images and compared them using standard metrics, to show how much the training data matters and whether virtual data is suitable for training neural networks.
